A dedicated study guide and technical reference for candidates preparing for the **American Petroleum Institute (API) 936 Refractory Personnel Certification Exam**, covering refractory materials, installation procedures, laboratory testing, inspection, and quality control.

---

## Exam Overview

The API 936 certification validates knowledge of refractory installation quality control, including the inspection, testing, and repair of monolithic refractory linings used in petroleum and petrochemical facilities.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based, closed-book, multiple-choice examination; available at in-person test centers or through remote proctoring.
- **Target Audience**: Refractory personnel, inspectors, contractors, manufacturers, and professionals involved in refractory installation and quality control.
- **Prerequisites**: API states that the certification is open to applicants with experience in the petroleum and petrochemical industries, as well as applicants seeking to gain industry experience.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Roles and Responsibilities

- Responsibilities of owners, inspectors, contractors, and manufacturers.
- Personnel duties related to refractory installation and quality control.
- Coordination and accountability throughout refractory work.

### 2. Laboratory Testing Procedures

- Selection and execution of refractory laboratory test methods.
- Interpretation of test results and applicable acceptance levels.
- Documentation of laboratory testing and material properties.

### 3. Materials and Installation Requirements

- Refractory types, material properties, packaging, and storage.
- Installation methods, including gunning, casting, and ramming.
- Lining design, anchors, sampling, mixing, and fiber additions.

### 4. Installation Inspection and Quality Control

- Material specifications, inspection procedures, and data collection.
- Visual and nondestructive inspection, acceptance criteria, and defect repair.
- Curing, dry-out requirements, and installation quality documentation.
